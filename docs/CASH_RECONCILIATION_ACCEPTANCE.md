# Cash reconciliation and EcoCash readiness

## PASS
- Provider wallet separates all-time confirmed cash receipts, historical cash-job commission, net cash earnings and personal electronic transfers.
- Receipts and commission derive from settled ledger records, not estimated current prices or rates.
- Actual cash counted is a local comparison only; it cannot mark receipts paid or alter wallet balances.
- Two arithmetic tests and eight payment policy tests passed.
- Signed-in desktop/mobile comparison checked: entering $85 against $0 recorded cash displayed a $85 difference. No page errors or mobile overflow.
- Existing provider EcoCash number/account-holder form remains available; added explicit warning never to enter PINs, passwords or OTPs.

## BLOCKED
- No EcoCash outbound connector, credentials or verified disbursement API is configured. A recipient’s EcoCash number is not sender authorization. No payout sent or marked paid.
- Genuine booking completion/cash receipt not tested: no identified real job/accounts or confirmation of actual work and receipt supplied.
- Physical cash in hand is provider-reported; no independent physical verification is claimed.
